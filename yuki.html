Yuki Tanaka

@yuki.irezumi @yuki.irezumi

One composition, read across the whole body

Irezumi-rooted large-scale work, planned in sessions.

Yuki studied traditional irezumi composition in Osaka for four years before bringing it stateside, focusing on how a single large-scale piece reads as one continuous image rather than a collection of separate tattoos.

She splits her time between full back pieces and smaller illustrative work that borrows the same sense of movement — water, wind, and figures caught mid-motion show up in almost everything she draws.

Large-scale projects are booked in multi-session arcs planned out from the first consult, usually four to eight sittings depending on size, so it's worth reaching out a few months ahead if you're thinking about a back piece or sleeve.
