Flash on the wall, ink that holds for decades

Traditional bones, color realism layered on top.

Desmond apprenticed in a street shop that did nothing but traditional flash for six years before he ever took a custom request. That flash-first training still shows in the sheets he hand-draws and pins up behind the front desk every few weeks — a rotating set anyone can walk in and pick straight off the wall.

He started layering in color realism about five years ago, and now splits his week between bold traditional linework and portrait-style color pieces, though he'll tell you the traditional work is what keeps his hand honest.

If you want something that reads clearly from across a room and still looks sharp in twenty years, he's the one to see — he won't talk you out of a design, but he will tell you straight if the size or placement won't hold up.
