Good to Know

The practical stuff, answered up front.

How do I actually book a session?

We don't run an online booking form. Head to the artist's profile page and message them directly on Instagram with your idea, placement, and size — they'll work out a time with you from there.

Do you take walk-ins?

When an artist has an open slot, yes — but most custom work is booked ahead of time directly with the artist rather than same-day.

Is there a deposit?

Yes — a $50 deposit holds your appointment and comes off the final price. It's non-refundable if you no-show or cancel with less than 48 hours' notice.

What's the age requirement?

18 and up, valid photo ID required. We don't tattoo minors, even with parental consent.

How should I prep for my appointment?

Sleep well, eat beforehand, stay hydrated, and skip the alcohol for 24 hours. Wear something that gives easy access to the area being worked on.
