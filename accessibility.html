Accessibility Statement

Last updated: September 2026

Elevated Ink Tattoo Co. wants this website to be usable by as many people as possible, including people with disabilities.

1. Our Commitment

We aim for this Site to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA as a general goal. This is an ongoing effort rather than a certified guarantee of full compliance.

2. Measures We've Taken

  • Semantic HTML structure, so screen readers and other assistive technology can navigate the page logically.
  • Descriptive alt text on the logo and other meaningful images.
  • A keyboard-operable menu, buttons, and links throughout the Site.
  • Labeled form fields wherever the Site collects input.
  • A responsive layout that adapts to phones, tablets, and desktop screens.

3. Known Limitations

Some elements — like the decorative background artwork and the hand-drawn gallery pieces — are presentational and don't carry individual text descriptions beyond the caption already shown with each piece. We're continuing to look for ways to improve accessibility across the Site.

4. Feedback

If you run into an accessibility barrier anywhere on this Site, please let us know at elevatedink2025@gmail.com or (681) 575-0008, and we'll do our best to address it.
