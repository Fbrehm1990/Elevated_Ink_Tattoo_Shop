Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Elevated Ink Tattoo Co. collects in connection with this website (the "Site") and how it's used.

1. Information We Don't Collect Through This Site

This Site does not have an online booking form, account creation, or payment processing. We don't collect your name, email, or payment information through the Site itself.

2. Information Collected When You Book an Appointment

When you reach out to book — typically by messaging an artist directly on Instagram — and an appointment is scheduled, your artist may enter your name, phone number, and notes about your appointment (such as placement, size, or reference details) into our internal scheduling system. This information is used solely to manage your appointment and contact you if plans change. Access to this system is restricted to signed-in artists, and each artist can only see and manage their own appointments.

3. Third-Party Services We Use

  • Instagram & Facebook: messages you send an artist or the shop through these platforms are governed by Meta's own privacy policy, not this one.
  • Google Fonts: this Site loads typefaces from Google, which may receive your IP address as part of that request, as with any site using Google Fonts.
  • Supabase: our appointment scheduling system is hosted by Supabase; appointment details entered by our artists are stored there securely.
  • Netlify: our website host, which like most hosting providers may log standard technical data (such as IP address and browser type) for security and performance purposes.

4. Cookies & Tracking

This Site does not use cookies, analytics scripts, or advertising trackers.

5. Data Security

Appointment information is stored in a password-protected system accessible only to signed-in artists. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold.

6. Children's Privacy

Our tattoo services are provided only to individuals 18 and older. This Site is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

7. Your Rights

If you'd like to know what information we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, email us at elevatedink2025@gmail.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Reach us at elevatedink2025@gmail.com or (681) 575-0008.
